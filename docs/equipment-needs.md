## Selection notes

- HDMI with Ethernet is a display-cable feature; record Ethernet network cables separately.
- Evaluate a UPS for brief backup and orderly shutdown. Longer operation may require a battery power station or generator, depending on the load and runtime requirement.
- Do not assign a backup-power model or cable quantity until the requirements are established.
- Record acquired equipment with its source and acquisition date. Do not count purchases as donated items.
