## Physical inspection recorded — October 5, 2026

Two additional operator-supplied photographs received on October 5 show the fan/air shroud, an empty blue 2.5-inch drive caddy, and the wireless/SSD labels. The operator reports that no 2.5-inch SATA drive is installed. The labels identify the wireless card as Dell DW1810 and the SSD as Toshiba KBG40ZNS128G, 128 GB. This inspection adds component identification; it does not establish SSD health, cooling performance, additional wireless tests, or completed reassembly.

Dell's [DW1810 wireless-module specifications](https://www.dell.com/support/manuals/en-us/inspiron-3881-desktop/inspiron-3881-setup-and-specifications/wireless-module?guid=guid-7dd4e94b-d18f-47a5-8d48-ab691dde331e) identify the card as Qualcomm QCA9377 with Wi-Fi 5 / 802.11ac and 2.4/5 GHz capability. Kioxia's [BG4 product brief](https://www.kioxia.com/content/dam/kioxia/shared/business/ssd/client-ssd/asset/productbrief/cSSD-BG4-product-brief.pdf) lists the matching KBG40ZNS128G as a 128 GB M.2 2230 PCIe Gen3 x4 NVMe SSD. These are published component specifications, not measured PC-001 throughput or proof that each capability was tested.
