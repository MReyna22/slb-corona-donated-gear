## Evidence and limits

The photographs establish component presence and readable labels. They do not establish that both memory modules are recognized, the drive is healthy, wireless networking works, the cooling system performs correctly, or the computer boots. The two populated memory slots do not establish a total RAM capacity.

Intel's [AC 7260 specifications](https://www.intel.com/content/www/us/en/products/sku/75439/intel-dual-band-wirelessac-7260/specifications.html) list 802.11ac, 2.4/5 GHz and integrated Bluetooth. These are manufacturer capabilities, not PC-002 test results.

Sensitive label regions are permanently obscured in the public copies, including device-specific identifiers, QR codes, and barcodes. Image metadata is removed. Model and capacity text are retained where readable. Untouched originals and full-resolution redacted copies remain private; public copies were resized to a 2048-pixel longest edge after permanent redaction.
